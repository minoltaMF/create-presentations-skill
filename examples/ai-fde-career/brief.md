# Presentation Brief

## Communication job

By the end, experienced product, engineering, and business professionals should understand that becoming an AI FDE means owning the path from an ambiguous business problem to adopted, measurable AI capability—and should be able to choose a practical first project.

## Context

- Use context: introductory promotion for an AI FDE course
- Audience: experienced product managers, engineers, and business professionals
- Page count: 6-page public Skill example
- Primary route: Native PPTX
- Additional route: HTML

## Inputs and evidence boundary

- The example uses an authorial framework rather than researched market statistics.
- No fictional salary, hiring, adoption, or performance data is presented as fact.
- The 30-day starter plan is practical guidance, not a guaranteed outcome.

## Visual direction

- 16:9
- Editorial paper with cobalt blue and warm amber
- Calm, modern, professional, and inventive
- Large audience-facing claims with varied page silhouettes
- Minimal page markers; no UI dashboard styling
